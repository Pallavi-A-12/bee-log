DESIGN

Designing interfaces people actually enjoy using

Great design isn't about adding more. It's about making the right things easier to understand.

AM
Alex Morgan Aug 22, 2026 · 7 min read
SHARE

Good interfaces have a quiet quality about them. They don't demand attention. Instead, they help people accomplish what they came to do.

As designers, it's tempting to focus on visual polish. Beautiful typography, gradients, animations and illustrations can make a product feel special.

Start with the user's goal

Before thinking about colors or components, understand what the person actually wants to accomplish. Every screen should have a clear purpose.

“Good design makes complexity feel simple.”

When an interface is built around a clear goal, decisions become easier. Remove anything that distracts from that goal and give important actions the attention they deserve.

Make the next step obvious

Users shouldn't have to stop and decode an interface. Strong hierarchy, meaningful labels and familiar patterns can guide people naturally through an experience.
